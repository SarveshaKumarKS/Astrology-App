<product_requirements>
The goal is to build a bilingual (Tamil/English) Android app for Tamil astrology, requiring monthly/annual subscriptions.

**Core Systems & Features:**

1.  **Vakkiam System (Traditional Astrological Calculations):**
    *   **Personal Horoscope (ஜாதகம் கணித்தல்):** Inputs: Name, Date/Time/Place of Birth, Lat/Long, Timezone, Time Correction. Outputs: Multi-tab report including Ascendant, Moon Sign, Star, Planetary Positions (table), Rasi/Navamsam Charts, Dasa Periods.
    *   **Marriage Compatibility (திருமணப் பொருத்தம் பார்த்தல்):** Inputs: 8 birth details for both male and female. Outputs: Multi-tab Compatibility Report with Star Matching and Dosha Analysis.
    *   **Saving & Managing Birth Profiles (ஜாதகங்களை சேமித்தல்):** Save and load user-generated birth profiles.
    *   **PDF Horoscope Reports (PDF ஜாதக அறிக்கை):** Generate shareable PDFs with traditional charts and Tamil text from generated horoscopes.

2.  **Thirukkanitham System (Astronomical Calculation Method):**
    *   **Personal Horoscope (ஜாதகம் கணித்தல்):** Similar to Vakkiam, but uses Thirukkanitham method for calculations.
    *   **Marriage Compatibility (திருமணப் பொருத்தம் பார்த்தல்):** Similar to Vakkiam, but uses Thirukkanitham method for calculations.
    *   **Daily Astrological Calendar (தினசரி பஞ்சாங்கம் பார்த்தல்):** Inputs: Date. Outputs: Panchangam details (Tithi, Nakshatram, Yogam, Karanam, Rahu Kalam).
    *   **Chart Display Styles (ராசி கட்ட அமைப்பு):** User can select North or South Indian chart styles from settings.

**Additional Requirements:**
*   **Localization:** UI must support Tamil and English.
*   **Subscription:** Monthly/annual payments using Razorpay (India-focused) test keys initially.
*   **MVP Priority:** Basic horoscope generation (Vakkiam and Thirukkanitham) and Marriage Compatibility (Vakkiam and Thirukkanitham) are the first working features.
*   **PDF:** Include traditional chart diagrams and Tamil text.
